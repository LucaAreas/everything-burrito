# Everything Burrito 🌯🥴

A bizarre Everything Burrito recipe inspired by *Adventure Time*.

This tiny, single-page HTML project presents an absurdly oversized burrito filled with rice, beans, beef, cheese, egg, pickles, potato chips, chocolate chips, strawberry jam, and more.

## View the recipe

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in any web browser to view the recipe.

Alternatively, from the project directory, start a local server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Ingredients

- 2 large flour tortillas
- ½ cup cooked rice
- ½ cup refried beans
- ½ cup shredded cheese
- 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons mustard
- 1 boiled egg, chopped
- ½ cup cooked ground beef
- 3 tablespoons diced pickles
- ¼ cup chopped onions
- ¼ cup crushed potato chips
- 2 tablespoons chocolate chips
- 1 tablespoon strawberry jam
- Hot sauce, to taste
- Salt and pepper

## Quick instructions

1. Place the tortillas on a flat surface.
2. Spread the refried beans and mayonnaise over each tortilla.
3. Add the rice, ground beef, cheese, egg, onions, and pickles.
4. Sprinkle with crushed potato chips.
5. Add mustard, hot sauce, chocolate chips, and strawberry jam.
6. Season with salt and pepper.
7. Fold and roll the tortillas.
8. Cook in a skillet for 2–3 minutes per side until golden brown.
