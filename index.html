Everything Burrito

An absurdly oversized burrito filled with practically everything you can imagine!

Ingredients

  • 2 large flour tortillas
  • ½ cup cooked rice
  • ½ cup fried beans
  • ½ cup shredded cheese
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons mustard
  • 1 boiled egg, chopped
  • ½ cup cooked ground beef
  • 3 tablespoons diced pickles
  • ¼ cup chopped onions
  • ¼ cup crushed potato chips
  • 2 table spoons chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon strawberry jam
  • Hot sauce (to taste)
  • A pinch of salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Place the tortillas on a flat surface.
  2. Spread the refried beans and mayonnaise over each tortilla.
  3. Add the rice, ground beef, cheese, boiled egg, onions and pickles.
  4. Sprinkle crushed potato chips on top for extra crunch.
  5. Add mustard, hot sauce, chocolate chips and strawberry jam.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Fold the sides inward and roll the tortillas into enormous burritos.
  8. Heat in a skillet for 2 to 3 minutes on each side until golden brown.

Final step.

Take a deep breath.

Look at your creation.

Question your life choices.

Enjoy your Everything Burrito!
